Privacy Policy for KB Masale Vyapar

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

Welcome to KB Masale Vyapar ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when using our mobile application (KB Masale Vyapar - Salesman Portal).

1. Information We Collect

To provide sales management and distribution features, our application collects the following information:

  • Account & Profile Information: Registered Salesman ID, Mobile Number, Email Address, and Login Credentials provided by your administrator.
  • Sales & Operational Data: Daily beat schedules, shop visit logs, order placement details, payment records, and product inventory metrics.
  • Device & Network Diagnostics: Network connection state (Wi-Fi/Cellular) and device sync statuses to facilitate offline data caching and online server synchronization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for business and operational purposes, including:

  • Authenticating salesmen and managing portal access.
  • Processing shop visits, order bookings, and sales reports.
  • Synchronizing offline data with the KB Masale server database when an internet connection is available.
  • Ensuring app performance, reliability, and security.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third parties or advertisers. Information is shared strictly with authorized server infrastructure for processing salesman operations.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data transmitted between the mobile application and server APIs is encrypted using secure HTTPS/TLS protocol. Offline data cached locally on the device is securely stored within the application's isolated storage environment.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain operational data as long as your salesman account remains active for business operations. Users or administrators may request account deactivation or data removal by contacting our support team at support@kbmasale.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is designed exclusively for enterprise sales personnel and business partners and does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
